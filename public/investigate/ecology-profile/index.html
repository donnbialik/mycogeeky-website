Plants recorded near collection sites

From iNaturalist plant observations within the chosen radius of each precise site. “Enrichment” compares each plant’s share of nearby records with its share within 5 km (median across sites, shown only when at least 3 sites have records to compare); above 1 means it is recorded near collections more often than in the surrounding area. With few sites, treat large values as leads, not findings. Absence of a record does not mean absence of the plant.

Mapped vegetation type

LANDFIRE 2024 Existing Vegetation Type, 30 m pixels, United States only. Mapped classes can differ from what is on the ground, especially at forest edges and in parks next to developed land.

Genus workbooktake these collections into a genus research spreadsheet

Already have a genus workbook? Copy the rows and paste them into the first empty row of its Site data tab (column A). The Collections tab fills its site columns from there by iNat number, so it doesn’t matter what order the rows are in. Starting a new one? Download the starter workbook: Collections comes filled with each observation’s iNat number, name, collector, date, voucher, sequence, locality and coordinates, Site data comes filled from this page, and the other tabs (described species, measurements and their automatic summaries, comparison matrix, type-sequence check, GenBank queue) are ready to fill in. It opens in Google Sheets and Claude Sheets; some automatic columns use functions that older versions of Excel don’t have, and Claude Sheets doesn’t keep the dropdown lists from the file.
