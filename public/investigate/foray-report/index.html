Species are distinct taxa identified to species or below; “other” are records identified only to genus or higher. Species per hour uses the time between the first and last photo of the foray and is shown when that is at least 15 minutes; long forays find more species simply because more ground is covered, so compare the per-hour column too. Rain is the total in the 14 days before, with its share of the 10-year normal for those days.
