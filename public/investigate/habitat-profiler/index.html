Habitat Profiler

What sets a species’ sites apart from the land around them, and where else nearby looks like that.

iNaturalist checking… LANDFIRE checking… Terrain checking… Soils checking…
  1. 1Pick a species (or a provisional name) and, if you like, a region
  2. 2Build the profile: each find is compared with points around it
  3. 3Read the signature, checking how strong each difference is
  4. 4Check an area: move the map to where you hunt and score it

It is not a probability that the species is there, and it says nothing about access: check that collecting is allowed before you go.
