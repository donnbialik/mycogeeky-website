ITS Bin Comparator

Compare ITS sequences across iNaturalist provisional bins and described species.

iNaturalist checking… GenBank checking… GBIF checking… UNITE manual import MyCoMap manual import BOLD manual import

1Add sequences

Open this search on iNaturalist
Open this search on NCBI

Searches ITS records 300–3000 bp. With "type material only" off, environmental and unvouchered records are included and flagged.

Use this for UNITE, MyCoMap, BOLD, PlutoF exports and your own unpublished sequences.

Activity

    Example data. The sequences shown are synthetic and generated for demonstration. They are not real observations.

    2Check your sequences

    Compare any sequences: click A or B on rows to pick them.

    Edit a group name to move a sequence. Untick to exclude it and note why. Region lengths come from conserved 5.8S and flanking SSU/LSU motifs and are approximate.

    Trim a large set

    One run can include up to 400 sequences; more than 150 takes longer. Choose what to drop. Sequences are excluded, not deleted, each with a note saying why, and Undo last trim puts them back.

    Identical sequences within a group

    Sorts the sequences of one group into haplotypes (sets that match 100%), then splits the group so you can compare the main haplotype with everything else.

    Optional
    Genus ITS standard
    How much ITS normally varies within and between species of a genus    
    
    
      
    Collects quality, named, vouchered ITS sequences for as many species of a genus as possible. It measures how much each species varies internally and how far each species is from its closest relative, then finds the cutoff that best separates the two. The Quick takeaway then judges your comparison against this genus standard instead of a single number for all fungi.
