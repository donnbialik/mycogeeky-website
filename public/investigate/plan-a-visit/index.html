    What turns up here

    “Years found” counts the years in which the species was recorded within the radius in this window. “Years looked” counts the years in which anyone recorded any fungi there in the same window. A species found in most of the years people looked is a regular; one found once may be chance, a misidentification or simply rare. Records show where people photographed fungi, not everything that grows there. Names and photos are not identifications or edibility advice: check every find yourself.
